# Turning Data into Decisions: My Junior Data Analyst Capstone Project



I'm excited to share my Junior Data Analyst capstone project, where I used Python, SQL, pandas, and data visualization to analyse retail sales data and identify business insights.



**Key findings from my analysis:**



- Analysed 4,998 retail sales records.

- Identified approximately KES 304.1 million in net sales.

- Calculated approximately KES 78.8 million in gross profit.

- Achieved a calculated gross margin of 25.93%.

- Developed eight SQL analyses to investigate business performance.

- Created analytical notebooks and visual reports to communicate findings.



**What I learned**



This project strengthened my practical skills in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL querying, Python programming, data visualization, and business reporting. It also helped me understand how data can support better decisions.
